# Coding-Quest

## 📊 Insights — what the contest data actually tells you

Switch to the **Insights** tab to get answers computed live, in your browser, from Codeforces' full contest history plus every upcoming contest on Codeforces, LeetCode and CodeChef. All times are in your own timezone.

| Insight | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| **Clash detector** | Which contests overlap in the next 14 days, and by how many minutes? |
| **Your next 14 days** | How busy is each day, by platform, and which days are free for upsolving? |
| **When contests actually happen** | Weekday × hour heatmap of the last 12 months: when should you keep your evenings free? |
| **Is Codeforces getting busier?** | Rounds per month over 36 months, with the year-on-year change |
| **Division mix** | What share of rounds are Div 2 / 3 / 4 / Educational, and how long each typically runs |
| **Cadence forecast** | From historical gaps: when is the next Div 3, Div 4 or Educational round likely, even before it's announced? |

Implementation: `insights.js` (vanilla JS, no libraries). Charts are hand-built SVG, and parallel Div 1 / Div 2 rounds are counted once per time slot so frequency stats aren't double-counted.

# Project Concept:

🔧 HTML: To structure and organize content on the platform.
🎨 CSS: To style and design the user interface, ensuring an attractive and user-friendly presentation.
